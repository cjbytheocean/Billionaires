# Billionaires

Instructions: 

Press "B", "G", "R" or "Y" to place blue, green, red, or yellow flags respectively for up to two flags per team. Out of each base, or billionaire, billions will spawn. Billions of a given color will gravitate towards the flags that are placed.

To move the flags after they are placed, continue to press the team's key while the mouse is hovered over where the flag's next location should be. Or, click and drag the flag to the desired location.

The goal is to rank up bases and destroy other billionaires. The billionaires rank up and get strong as they destroy their opponents, so watch out for attackers! The last billionaire standing wins.
